About SecureU

SecureU gives students one place to get safety help and track their wellbeing - built to keep working even when internet is weak or unavailable, since that's exactly when it matters most.

All data you enter (check-ins, reports, profile) is stored only on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you set up the optional email notification described in the project README.
